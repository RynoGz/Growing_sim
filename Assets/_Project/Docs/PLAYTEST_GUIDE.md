# Growveld prototype playtest

## First farm

1. Press `T`, open **Shop**, and buy seeds, nutrients, a watering can, grow pot, drying rack, and storage bin. Add a prefab grow room and grow light for the indoor route.
2. Deliveries arrive in five in-game minutes and enter the hotbar automatically.
3. Select equipment, close the tablet, press `B`, and place it on owned land. Use `R` to rotate.
4. Aim at an empty grow position and press `E` with seeds in inventory.
5. Keep water and nutrients healthy. Outdoor crops need daylight; indoor crops need a room, good humidity, and grow-light coverage.
6. Harvest with `E`, physically carry the fresh batch to a drying rack, wait until Dried, then carry it to storage.
7. Open the tablet's **Sell** tab and sell all stored stock. Quality changes yield and price.

## Useful controls

- `H`: full controls reference
- `Escape`: pause or back
- `F5`: manual save
- `F9`: load latest save
- `E`: interact, pick up, drop, or transfer
- `T`: business tablet

The game also autosaves every two minutes and on exit.
