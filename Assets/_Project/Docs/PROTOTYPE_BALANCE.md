# Prototype balance

Phase 23 uses fictional values designed for a short, playable prototype loop.

## Starting paths

- Starting balance: R30,000
- Outdoor starter kit: R8,310 (seed, nutrients, watering can, pot, drying rack, and storage)
- Indoor starter kit: R29,510 (outdoor kit plus grow light and prefab grow room)
- Expansion plots: R15,000 and R18,000

The outdoor path leaves expansion capital but grows more slowly. The indoor path uses almost all starting capital but provides controlled, faster growth.

## Crop and sale

- Ideal base cycle: 30 real minutes of growth plus 10 real minutes of drying
- Base yield: 0.65 kg; excellent care can raise this to roughly 0.73 kg
- Sale rate: R1,000/kg before quality
- Quality multipliers: Low 0.70, Standard 1.00, Premium 1.30, Top Grade 1.60
- An excellent crop sells for roughly R1,165 before recurring costs

## Utilities

- Grow light: 1.2 kW, scheduled for 18 simulated hours per day
- Electricity: R3.25/kWh, or about R70.20 per light per day
- Watering: 15 L at R0.06/L, or R0.90 per use

Equipment is reusable. Seeds and nutrients remain the primary recurring purchase costs. Bills may make the balance negative; sales still work, but new purchases are blocked until the balance recovers.
